 Kivu Roots – E-Commerce UI/UX Design (Figma)

•	Selected Application
Kivu Roots – an Online Shopping / E-Commerce application connecting Rwandan artisans and coffee farmers around Lake Kivu directly to customers.

•	Problem Statement
Rwandan artisans and smallholder coffee farmers struggle to reach buyers beyond their local market, limiting their income and visibility. Kivu Roots is a web application that lets customers browse and purchase authentic handmade crafts and locally grown coffee directly from artisans, while giving sellers a simple, direct channel to showcase their goods. This matters because it creates a digital bridge between rural producers and a wider customer base, increasing fair income for the community.
•	User Persona
- Occupation: Marketing professional living in Kigali
- Goals: Find authentic, high-quality Rwandan crafts and coffee quickly online; support local artisans
- Challenges/Frustrations: Hard to verify authenticity when buying from unofficial social media pages; slow or unclear checkout processes

•	User Flow
Login/Register → Home → Shop → Product Detail → Add to Cart → Cart → Checkout → Payment Confirmation

•	Explanation: 
The user starts by logging in or registering, lands on the Home page featuring the brand story and highlighted products, browses the full Shop catalog, views details of a specific product, adds it to their cart, reviews their cart, and completes checkout to confirm payment.

•	Design Explanation
- Wireframes (low-fidelity):  Built as simple gray-box layouts to establish structure — Login/Register, Home, Shop, Product Detail, and Cart screens.
- High-Fidelity Screens:  Styled versions of the wireframes using a warm, earthy color palette (terracotta, deep green, cream) reflecting the "Heart of Kivu" brand identity, with consistent typography, spacing, and product imagery placeholders.
- Prototype: Screens are connected with clickable interactions — navigation menu links, product clicks leading to Product Detail, Add to Cart and Checkout button flows — to simulate real app navigation.

•	Features Implemented
- Login/Register screen
- Home page with hero banner and featured products
- Shop page with full product listing (Beaded Necklace, Woven Sisal Basket, Kivu Blend Coffee, Imigongo Wall Art, Banana Fiber Placemats)
- Product Detail page with image, name, price, and description
- Shopping Cart with item list, total, and checkout button
- Navigation bar consistent across all screens

•	Accessibility Considerations
- Readable font sizes for body text and headings
- High color contrast between text and backgrounds
- Clear, consistent navigation placement across all screens
- Simple, user-friendly layouts with clear call-to-action buttons (Add to Cart, Checkout)

•	Challenges Faced
 Balancing screen time between group members and Learning Figma's frame and prototyping tools for the first time.

•	Conclusion

 This project strengthened our understanding of UI/UX design principles, from identifying a real-world problem to translating it into user-centered wireframes and high-fidelity designs. Designing Kivu Roots helped us practice building an e-commerce experience that is both visually appealing and easy to navigate.
